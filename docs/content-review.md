## 2. History timeline

The 12 entries are written from facts in the History, Traditions and profile documents. Wording is mine.

- **1929, Government College Ibadan is Founded:** Government College Ibadan is founded, beginning as a teacher training school established by Selwyn Macgregor Grier, Director of Education for the Southern Provinces. V. B. V. Powell is posted there to teach Mathematics and History.
- **1930, Grier House is Founded:** In January 1930 Government College Ibadan becomes a secondary school and Grier House is founded as its second House, after Swanston House, with V. B. V. Powell as its first Housemaster. He names the House after Selwyn Macgregor Grier and gives it its Crest, Colour, Motto, House Prayer and House Supper.
- **1933, The Grier Cup is Started:** V. B. V. Powell starts the Grier Cup athletic competition for secondary schools. Around the same year, the tradition of moonlight stories begins in Grier House.
- **1935, Red Letter Day:** News reaches Grier House that Selwyn Macgregor Grier has been made Governor of the Windward Islands. The day is marked on the calendar with a red circle.
- **1940, Wartime Exile:** During the Second World War, Griersons leave their dormitory and live in exile in Lagos and at St Theresa's Convent, Oke-Ado, until 1945.
- **1945, The Cairn:** A rock is placed in front of the old Grier House to mark the end of the Second World War and the return of Griersons to their dormitory.
- **1952, Grier House Moves to a New Site:** Grier House moves from its first site close to the main gate to a new site near Field House.
- **1958, First Nigerian Principal:** Gilbert Nixon Imafidon Enobakhare, a Grierson of the 1933 set and former Housemaster, becomes the first Nigerian Principal of Government College Ibadan.
- **1982, Boarding is Abolished:** All students are moved out of Grier House following an Oyo State Government policy abolishing boarding houses. The building falls into disrepair.
- **1986, Wole Soyinka Wins the Nobel Prize in Literature:** Wole Soyinka, for whom Grier House was the last House he called home, becomes the first Black African awarded the Nobel Prize in Literature.
- **2024, A New Grier House:** A new Grier House building is built through the donation of Wale Babalakin, a Grierson of the 1971 set. The first group of boys moves in during 2025.
- **2026, The Grier Museum and Library Opens:** The Grier Museum and Library is established in October 2026 as a legacy project of the Grier History and Traditions Committee.
